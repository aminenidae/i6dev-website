How can we help?

A fun app didn't lock when my time ran out.

iOS sometimes can't block an app that is already open when its time runs out. Close the app fully: swipe up from the bottom of the screen, then swipe the app away. When you open it again, the block screen appears.

Why did my change say "Starts tomorrow"?

That's on purpose: today keeps the plan you set, so a weak moment can't undo it. Every change in Settings, like free time, quick checks, the daily max, Grow first, the earning rate or Off-limits times, starts the next day. The plan at the top of Settings shows what tomorrow looks like, and Reset drops changes that are waiting.

On the Fun tab, adding apps or websites applies right away. Removing a fun app, unblocking a website or turning browsers back on waits until tomorrow, and you can keep today's list until then.

How do I block the website version of an app?

Open the Fun tab and tap Add or Remove Apps. Websites are listed near the bottom of the picker, for example tiktok.com under TikTok. Tick them along with the app. iOS only lists websites you've visited in Safari, so open the site once first if it's missing. You can also manage websites in Fun › Block websites & browsers, where Premium can block every browser too.

My minutes don't exactly match iOS Screen Time.

Tradeoff counts in whole minutes as iOS reports them, so a difference of a minute or two per app is normal. iOS sometimes reports growth time a few minutes late, and late minutes catch up on their own. Home shows when growth time was last counted. If the gap stays large, open Tradeoff so it can refresh, and email us if it continues.

Why do some Health minutes not count? (Premium)

Only time your iPhone, Apple Watch or a workout app measured counts, not entries typed into Health by hand. Each day, up to 90 minutes of movement, 30 mindful minutes and 30 minutes in daylight count, and a night of 7 hours or more adds 30 minutes. A minute counts once, even if two things measured it. Minutes you don't add from the Health tab are gone at midnight.

Seeing 0? In the Health app, go to Sharing › Apps › Tradeoff and turn everything on.

Why do alerts say "a fun app" instead of the app's name?

To protect your privacy, Apple doesn't share which apps you use with app developers. Inside the app, iOS draws the real names and icons for you. Alerts sent in the background can't, so they use neutral wording.

What's free and what's Premium?

Free, forever: one fun app and one growth app, with free time, Grow first, earned time, quick checks and website blocking. On the free plan you choose your apps in Settings › Plan › Choose your free apps.

Premium: every fun and growth app, earning with Apple Health, 30-day insights and blocking every browser. It's a yearly or monthly subscription, and the app shows the price in your currency before you subscribe. New subscribers get a 1-week free trial on the yearly plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Tradeoff, or open Settings › Plan › Manage Subscription in the app. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I remove all my data?

Delete the Tradeoff app. Everything it stores on your iPhone is removed with it. If you created a profile, delete it first: open Settings › Account, tap your profile, then Delete Account. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete your profile.

To also remove Tradeoff's access, go to iPhone Settings › Screen Time, and in the Health app to Sharing › Apps › Tradeoff.
