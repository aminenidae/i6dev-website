How can we help?

A limited app didn't lock when my time ran out.

iOS sometimes can't block an app that is already open when its time runs out. Close the app fully: swipe up from the bottom of the screen, then swipe the app away. When you open it again, the block screen appears.

Why did my change say "Starts tomorrow"?

That's on purpose. Changes that make your rules easier, like a lower goal, a higher daily maximum, longer allowed hours, or unblocking an app or website, take effect the next day. Stricter changes apply right away. It stops a weak moment from undoing your limits. You can cancel a pending change from the app's page in Tradeoff.

How do I block the website version of an app?

Open the Limited tab and tap Add or Remove Apps. Websites are listed near the bottom of the picker, for example tiktok.com under TikTok. Tick them along with the app. iOS only lists websites you've visited in Safari, so open the site once first if it's missing. To block every browser, go to Limited › Block websites & browsers.

My minutes don't exactly match iOS Screen Time.

Tradeoff counts in whole minutes as iOS reports them, so a difference of a minute or two per app is normal. If the gap is larger, open Tradeoff so it can refresh. If it continues, email us and we'll help.

Why does Tradeoff sometimes show "Limited app" instead of an app's name?

To protect your privacy, Apple doesn't share which apps you use with app developers. Inside the app, iOS draws the real names and icons for you. Alerts sent in the background can't, so they use neutral wording.

What's free and what's Premium?

Free: one limited app and one productive app, with goals, daily maximums, allowed hours and website blocking. Premium: every app, 30-day insights with time reclaimed, and browser blocking. Premium is US$4.99 a month or US$29.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. On the free plan you choose your free apps in Settings › Plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Tradeoff, or open Settings › Plan › Manage Subscription in the app. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I remove all my data?

Delete the Tradeoff app. Everything it stores is on your iPhone and is removed with it. To also remove its Screen Time access, go to iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
