Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tradeoff app ("Tradeoff", "the App") handles, published by i6dev.ca ("we", "us"). The short version: Tradeoff works fully without an account. Your app activity (which apps you use and when) stays on your iPhone. To improve the App, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that aren't linked to you, and you can turn these off in Settings. If you choose to create a free profile, we store your email address and a summary of your progress so you can get them back.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps the following on your iPhone, in storage that only Tradeoff and its built-in extensions can read:

  • The apps and websites you choose as limited or productive. Apple provides these to the App as anonymous tokens. Apple does not tell us their names.
  • Minutes used on the apps you chose, measured by Apple's Screen Time framework, with a daily history of about 30 days.
  • Your rules: which productive apps earn time for each fun app, the minimum and earning rate for each, time limits, daily maximums, allowed hours, and changes scheduled for the next day.
  • Your setup answers, such as your own estimate of daily time on fun apps, used for your balance report and in Insights.
  • Your app preferences, such as alert settings and your first name if you type one during setup (it stays on your iPhone).
  • Technical logs about how tracking is working, used to diagnose problems.

If you don't create a profile, none of this is uploaded to us or to any server we control, except the anonymous usage statistics and crash reports described below.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, the App sends anonymous statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase and crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics. They are not linked to your name, email or profile, and are never used for advertising.

  • How you use the App: which screens you view, setup steps you complete, your setup answers (such as the kinds of apps you want to balance and your own time estimates), changes to your rules (for example "earning rate changed", not which apps), how many apps you chose, daily totals of minutes on productive and fun apps, and subscription events such as viewing the subscription screen or starting a trial.
  • Technical details: device model, iOS version, App version, language, and country or region (derived from your IP address, which is not stored).
  • An anonymous app ID generated by Firebase for this installation, so events from the same installation can be grouped. It resets if you delete and reinstall the App.
  • Crash reports: technical information about the crash and the state of the App at that moment.

These statistics never include the names of apps or websites you use, what you do in them, your email, or any advertising identifier. The App uses the version of Firebase Analytics that does not collect the advertising identifier, and it does not ask to track you.

Turning it off: Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data. When off, the App stops sending usage statistics and crash reports.

Apple Ads: if you installed Tradeoff after tapping an Apple Ads ad, the App asks Apple, through Apple's privacy-preserving AdServices framework, which campaign the install came from (campaign, ad group and keyword numbers). Apple does not share who you are, and no tracking permission is needed. We use this only to measure our own ads.

If you create a profile (optional)

You can create a free profile with Sign in with Apple or with a sign-in link sent to your email. There are no passwords. A profile saves your progress so you can restore it, and lets us email you. When you have a profile, we store:

  • Your email address and how you signed in. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's private relay address, never your real one.
  • A backup of your settings and rules: earning rates, daily goals and limits, allowed hours and alert preferences. The apps in your rules are stored only as Apple's anonymous tokens; no app names are uploaded. Custom names you give apps are not uploaded.
  • Your daily record: for each day, total minutes on productive apps, total minutes on limited apps, and whether you met your goal. Not which apps, and not when during the day.
  • Your answer to the "time on distracting apps" question, so Insights can be restored.
  • Basic account details: when the profile was created, when the App last saved, and the App version.
  • Your email choice: whether you agreed to receive tips and product news, with the exact wording you agreed to and when.
  • A discount code, if we sent you one (see below).

We use this information only to restore your progress, to show your record in the App, to send the emails described below, and to answer you when you contact us. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or combine it with information from other companies.

Emails

  • Sign-in emails. If you sign in by email, we send you a one-time sign-in link.
  • One-time discount. If you leave the subscription screen without subscribing and accept our one-time offer, creating a profile gives you a single-use Apple offer code. We show it in the App and email it to you once.
  • Tips and product news only if you turn this on. It is off unless you choose it. You can turn it off any time in the App (Profile) or by emailing tradeoffapp@i6dev.ca.

Emails are sent from tradeoffapp@i6dev.ca.

Information we do not collect

  • Your name, phone number or payment details
  • Which apps or websites you use, by name
  • What you do inside apps, your messages, photos or contacts
  • Your browsing history
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers

The App contains no advertising, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The only analytics are the anonymous statistics described above.

Services that process information for us

Google Firebase

Profiles, sign-in and saved progress are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), operated by Google LLC on our behalf. The data is stored in the United States. Google may process technical information, such as your IP address, to provide these services securely. These services are used only if you create a profile.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (described above) are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, and a copy of the anonymous statistics is kept in Google BigQuery for our own analysis. Google processes this data on our behalf; we do not allow it to be used for advertising.

IONOS

Discount and other emails we send are delivered through our email provider, IONOS.

Apple

Screen Time: Tradeoff uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to measure time on the apps you choose and to block apps and websites. This processing happens on your device under Apple's control. You can turn off Tradeoff's access at any time in iPhone Settings › Screen Time.

Sign in with Apple: if you use it, Apple confirms your identity and gives us your email address or a private relay address.

Subscriptions: Tradeoff Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing. We never see your payment details. The App only receives from Apple whether your subscription is active.

App icons: if iOS provides an app's public identifier, the App may ask Apple's public App Store lookup service for that app's icon. No information about you is attached to that request.

Notifications: alerts such as "Your fun app is unlocked" are created on your iPhone. They are not sent through our servers.

Diagnostic reports you choose to send

The App can export a diagnostic report if you decide to share one with us, for example by email. A report contains technical tracking logs and minutes used on your chosen apps, identified only by anonymous IDs. We use it solely to investigate the problem you reported and delete it when it is no longer needed. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • On your iPhone, daily usage history older than about 30 days is removed automatically. Everything else stays until you delete the App. Deleting Tradeoff removes all of its data from your device.
  • Your profile data is kept while you have a profile. You can delete your profile at any time in the App (Settings › Account, then Delete Account). This permanently deletes your profile and saved progress from our servers straight away. If you used Sign in with Apple, it also disconnects Tradeoff from your Apple ID.
  • Signing out keeps your profile so you can sign back in later; it does not delete it.
  • Copies of emails we send are deleted automatically within 7 days of sending.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months, then deleted. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Because they aren't linked to you, we can't find or delete one person's statistics on request; deleting and reinstalling the App starts a new anonymous ID, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • If you received a discount code, we keep a record that the code was used up, so it isn't handed out twice. It is no longer linked to your email once your profile is deleted.
  • To also remove Tradeoff's Screen Time access, go to iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing tradeoffapp@i6dev.ca. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Tradeoff is designed for adults managing their own screen time. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created a profile, email us and we will delete it.

Security

On your device, data stays in the App's private storage, protected by iOS. Profile data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in Google Firebase, where access rules let each person read and change only their own profile.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect, and mention it in the App Store release notes.
