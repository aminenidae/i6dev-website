Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tradeoff app ("Tradeoff", "the App") handles, published by i6dev.ca ("we", "us"). The short version: Tradeoff has no account, and the information it uses stays on your iPhone. We do not receive it.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps the following on your iPhone, in storage that only Tradeoff and its built-in extensions can read:

  • The apps and websites you choose as limited or productive. Apple provides these to the App as anonymous tokens. Apple does not tell us their names.
  • Minutes used on the apps you chose, measured by Apple's Screen Time framework, with a daily history of about 30 days.
  • Your rules: daily goals, earning rates, daily maximums, allowed hours, and changes scheduled for the next day.
  • Your setup answers, such as your own estimate of daily time on distracting apps, used to show "time reclaimed" in Insights.
  • Custom names you give to apps, and your app preferences such as alert settings.
  • Technical logs about how tracking is working, used to diagnose problems.

None of this is uploaded to us or to any server we control.

Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details
  • What you do inside apps, your messages, photos or contacts
  • Your browsing history
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers

The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Apple services the App relies on

Screen Time

Tradeoff uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to measure time on the apps you choose and to block apps and websites. This processing happens on your device under Apple's control. You can turn off Tradeoff's access at any time in iPhone Settings › Screen Time.

Subscriptions

Tradeoff Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing. We never see your payment details. The App only receives from Apple whether your subscription is active.

App Store search

If you use "Rename" on an app and search for its name, the text you type is sent to Apple's public App Store search service so the App can suggest matching names and icons. No information about you is attached to that search.

Notifications

Alerts such as "Goal reached" are created on your iPhone. They are not sent through our servers.

Diagnostic reports you choose to send

The App can export a diagnostic report if you decide to share one with us, for example by email to support. A report contains technical tracking logs and minutes used on your chosen apps, identified only by anonymous IDs. We use it solely to investigate the problem you reported and delete it when it is no longer needed. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • Daily usage history older than about 30 days is removed automatically.
  • Everything else stays on your iPhone until you delete the App. Deleting Tradeoff removes all of its data from your device.
  • To also remove its Screen Time access, go to iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
  • To ask about a diagnostic report you sent us, email support@i6dev.ca. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Tradeoff is designed for adults managing their own screen time. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Data stays in the App's private storage on your device, protected by iOS. Because we do not hold your data, there is no account or server of ours that could be breached.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect, and mention it in the App Store release notes.
