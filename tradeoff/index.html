Earn your screen time.
Spend it on what matters first.

Tradeoff locks the apps you want to use less until you've spent time on the ones that are good for you. Read, learn or train first, then scroll with a clear conscience.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

How it works

Set a daily goal

Choose your productive apps, like reading, learning or fitness, and how many minutes a day unlock your limited apps.

Earn, then enjoy

Reach your goal and your limited apps unlock. Every productive minute earns time on them, and they lock again when it runs out.

No easy way out

Stricter rules apply instantly. Easier ones wait until tomorrow, so a weak moment at 11 PM can't undo your limits.

Websites too

Block the website version of your limited apps, or every browser, so Safari isn't a back door.

Private by design

No account. Built on Apple's Screen Time framework, and everything stays on your iPhone. We never see which apps you use.

Free to start

One limited app and one productive app are free forever. Tradeoff Premium unlocks every app, 30-day insights and browser blocking.
