Your rhythm

How a day works

Set it up once. No timers to start and no tasks to tick off: when you've done the time, your fun apps open.

  1. Free time

    Your first 30 minutes of fun every day are on us.

  2. Grow first

    After that, your fun apps open once you've spent time in a growth app.

  3. Earned time

    Every minute of growth earns more fun, at the rate you choose.

  4. Quick checks

    A few short looks a day for anything urgent while your fun apps are locked.

  5. Off-limits

    Optional quiet times for sleep or work, when nothing opens your fun apps.

Hard to fake. Easy to keep.

Measured by iOS

Tradeoff uses Apple's Screen Time technology to measure the time you actually spend in your growth apps. Nothing to fake.

Small steps that stick

Start from the time you spend on your apps today and pick a goal you can keep. Your shift shows the change from your own starting point.

Built for weaker moments

Changes to your day start tomorrow, so today keeps the plan you set. An optional daily max caps fun time across all your fun apps.

No way around it

Block the websites of the apps you limit, so they don't just move to Safari. With Premium, hide browsers completely.

See your real mix

Insights shows your fun and growth time, day by day, measured by iOS.

Private by design

Your app activity and Health data stay on your iPhone. Apple doesn't tell us which apps you use. No ads, and nothing is used for tracking.
