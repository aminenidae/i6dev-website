About Tradeoff

In one line

Tradeoff is a screen time app that doesn't want people off their phones: time in apps that make them better earns time in the apps they enjoy.

Short description · 60 words

Tradeoff is an iPhone app for adults that changes the mix of their screen time instead of cutting it. Time in the growth apps they choose, such as reading, language learning, Bible or study, earns time in the fun apps they enjoy. Apple's Screen Time technology measures the minutes on the device, so there is nothing to log and nothing to fake.

Long description · 180 words

Most people have apps that would make them better (a reading app, a language course, a Bible plan) downloaded with good intentions and rarely opened. Tradeoff brings those apps back into the day without taking anything away.

Users pick their fun apps and their growth apps once, then set a goal that starts from where they are today. Each day begins with some free time in the fun apps. After that, the fun apps open once the day's growth goal is met, and every further minute of growth earns more fun at a rate the user sets. A few quick checks cover anything urgent, and optional Off-limits times keep sleep and work quiet, with a countdown on the Lock Screen.

Progress is measured against each person's own starting point, not an ideal: Today's mix shows growth beside fun, and a weekly story shows how the mix moved. With Premium, walks, workouts, mindful minutes, daylight and sleep measured by iPhone or Apple Watch add more fun time through Apple Health.

Which apps someone uses never leaves their iPhone, and there are no ads.

How it works

The words in bold are the names used in the app.

  • Growth apps and fun apps

    Users choose both lists once. iOS measures the time in each, so there are no timers to start and nothing to tick off.

  • Free time, then Grow first

    Every day starts with some free time in the fun apps. After that, Grow first: the fun apps open once the day's growth goal is met.

  • Earned time

    Each minute of growth past the goal earns more fun at a rate the user chooses. All fun apps share one balance, which resets at midnight.

  • Quick checks and Off-limits

    A few short quick checks a day for anything urgent. Optional Off-limits times for sleep or work, with a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

  • Small steps, measured from the start

    Today's mix shows growth beside fun, and Your shift compares the week with the user's own starting point. A weekly story sums up the week every Sunday.

  • Apple Health · Premium

    Walks, workouts, mindful minutes, daylight and sleep measured by iPhone, Apple Watch or workout apps add more fun time, within daily caps. Entries typed into Health by hand don't count.

The story

Most screen time apps treat the phone as the problem. Tradeoff treats the mix as the problem. People already own the apps they wish they used more, often paid for: the language course, the reading app, the Bible plan. Tradeoff puts those apps first in the day and keeps the fun ones, so the habit grows in small steps instead of a cut that rarely lasts.

Who makes it

i6 Development

Tradeoff is made by Ameen, a solo iOS developer and parent in Canada, under the name i6 Development. Before Tradeoff came Tic Lock, a screen time app for kids where learning apps earn time on games. Tradeoff takes the same trade to adults, who set their own rules.

In the founder's words

“I first built a screen time app for my kids, where learning apps earn time on games. Then I realized the same mechanism works for adults, so I built Tradeoff and tested it on myself and on my wife. My Spanish keeps improving, and my time on social media has gotten much better.”

“I didn't want my phone taken away. I like my fun apps. I just wanted the apps that make me better to get their turn first.”
